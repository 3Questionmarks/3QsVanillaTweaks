# Corpse Lily

- Gains **90%** damage per additional stack, up from **60%**.
- Cloud growth gains **1%** per additional stack for each **1%** holdout charge; maximum cloud size remains limited by the holdout radius.
- Damage can be displayed per tick instead of per second.

# Earthbound Vessel

- Grants 7 armour per unused rechargeable jump, up from 5.

# Stained Scimitar

- Grants **5% (+5% per stack)** lifesteal, up from **1%** (+**1%**).

# Vengeful Spirit

- No longer consumes a stack when its spirit dies.
- Grants 1 spirit per item stack.
- Spirits respawn after 30 seconds and absorb **30%** of incoming damage, down from **50%**.
- Allied deaths grant Vengeful while holding the item, including the spirit's death, without requiring the item to transform first.
