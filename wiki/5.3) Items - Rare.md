# Dio's Best Friend

- Consumed copies regenerate at the start of the next stage.

# Laser Scope

- The first Laser Scope also grants **10%** critical strike chance.

# Wake of Vultures

- Adds **30%** damage against elites. Stolen powers last 10 seconds, plus 5 seconds per extra stack.

- Adds Wandering Chef recipes: combine 1 Wake of Vultures with 1 ingredient below to produce 1 aspect.

| Additional ingredient (choose one) | Aspect |
| --- | --- |
| Halcyon Seed, Pearl or Irradiant Pearl | Aurelionite's Blessing |
| Runald's Band or Frost Relic | Her Biting Embrace |
| Rejuvenation Rack, Seed of Life or Lepton Daisy | His Reassurance |
| Kjaro's Band, Ignition Tank or Molotov (6-Pack) | Ifrit's Distinction |
| Any Hallowed item | N'kuhana's Rebirth |
| N'kuhana's Opinion or any Hex | N'kuhana's Retort |
| Box of Dynamite, Orphaned Core or Empathy Cores | Of One Mind |
| Any Lunar item | Shared Design |
| Royal Capacitor, Unstable Tesla Coil or Faulty Conductor | Silence Between Two Strikes |
| Unstable Transmitter, Old War Stealthkit or Shadow Cloak | Spectral Circlet |
| Any Void item | Entropic Fracture (requires ZetAspects) |

- ZetAspects recipes use its aspect item/equipment mode. Its Wake combat balance remains controlled by ZetAspects.

# Aegis

- Adds **25%** maximum barrier per stack.

# Alien Head

- Becomes uncommon and reduces cooldowns by **15%** per stack (down from **25%**).

# Ben's Raincoat

- Starts with 2 debuff blocks (up from 1), plus 1 per extra stack.
- Blocking grants **15%** barrier (up from **10%**) and 0.25 seconds of debuff immunity.
- Each block starts or resets the recharge timer; remaining blocks stay usable, and recharge restores all blocks.

# Brilliant Behemoth

- Explosions gain **20%** damage per extra stack.

# Ceremonial Dagger

- Daggers deal **200%** damage per stack (up from **150%**) and no longer trigger item effects.

# Frost Relic

- Gains **200%** base tick damage per extra stack. Damage ticks no longer trigger item effects.

# Growth Nectar

- Grants **5%** stats per qualifying buff (up from **4%**).

# Happiest Mask

- Kills summon an invulnerable ghost of your survivor, lasting for the stage. It deals **300%** damage (+**150%** per extra stack), credits kills to you and does not inherit items. Limited to one ghost.

# Resonance Disc

- Using primary, secondary, utility and special once each charges the disc. There is no time limit by default. Its attacks no longer trigger item effects. The optional kill-based mode allows 9 seconds for kills to contribute charge.

# Runic Lens

- Meteor damage gains **150%** per extra stack for every **100%** triggering attack damage (up from **50%**). Meteors no longer trigger item effects.

# Sentient Meat Hook

- Proc coefficient is 0.5 (up from 0.33).

# Shattering Justice

- Hits on an already pulverized enemy remove another 2 armour (+2 per extra stack) and refresh the debuff. Duration stays at 8 seconds instead of increasing with stacks.

# Sonorous Whispers

- Elite drop chance gains **2.5%** per extra stack (up from **1%**).

# Substandard Duplicator

- Temporary items last 30 seconds longer (+10 seconds per extra stack), added to the configured base duration.

# Symbiotic Scorpion

- Skill hits poison enemies instead of permanently reducing armour. Damage over time gains **1%** damage per **1%** missing target health, per item stack.

# Orphaned Core

- Automatically cleanses the owner and nearby allies every 30 seconds (-**10%** time per extra stack), replacing the pet-triggered cleanse. Still inherits movement items by default; inheritance can be set to None, Movement or All.
