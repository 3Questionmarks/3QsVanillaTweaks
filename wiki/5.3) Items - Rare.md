# Wake of Vultures

- Adds Wandering Chef recipes: combine 1 Wake of Vultures with 1 ingredient below to produce 1 aspect.

| Additional ingredient (choose one) | Aspect |
| --- | --- |
| Halcyon Seed, Pearl or Irradiant Pearl | Aurelionite's Blessing |
| Runald's Band or Frost Relic | Her Biting Embrace |
| Rejuvenation Rack, Seed of Life or Lepton Daisy | His Reassurance |
| Kjaro's Band, Ignition Tank or Molotov (6-Pack) | Ifrit's Distinction |
| Any Hallowed item | N'kuhana's Rebirth |
| N'kuhana's Opinion or any Hex | N'kuhana's Retort |
| Box of Dynamite, Orphaned Core or Empathy Cores | Of One Mind |
| Any Lunar item | Shared Design |
| Royal Capacitor, Unstable Tesla Coil or Faulty Conductor | Silence Between Two Strikes |
| Unstable Transmitter, Old War Stealthkit or Shadow Cloak | Spectral Circlet |
| Any Void item | Entropic Fracture (requires ZetAspects) |

- ZetAspects recipes use its aspect item/equipment mode. Its Wake combat balance remains controlled by ZetAspects.
