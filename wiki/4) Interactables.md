# Teleporter

- Increases the holdout radius to 90 metres.
- After the boss is defeated, the radius expands to cover effectively the whole map and charging becomes twice as fast.
- The Soul-binding ritual and overcharge phase retain their normal charging speed.

# Teleporter Rewards

- In multiplayer, each player gets a separate void potential pickup with the same set of rewards and can claim every item in their own pickup.
- Drifter's Tinker cannot reroll these potentials by default.
- Each Horde of Many reward has a 25% chance to become a bundle of 3 random Common items.

# Barrels and Stalks

- Normal barrels award $8 and 4 XP before scaling. Stalks award $25 and 0 XP before scaling.
- Both scale like a Small Chest by default, including multiplayer scaling. Vanilla linear scaling is available as an alternative.

# Cloaked Chests

- Glimmer every 4 seconds by default. The glimmer and interval are configurable.
- Default loot weights are 80% Uncommon and 20% Legendary. Other item pools can be enabled by changing their weights.

# Shrine Of Blood

- Awards gold equal to the health lost, plus the current cost of one Small Chest.

# Shrine Of Chance

- Starting rewards are 50% nothing, 40% Common, 9% Uncommon and 1% Legendary; equipment is removed from the pool.
- Failures improve reward quality. While Common items remain above 1%, 10 percentage points move from Common into Uncommon and Legendary in a 9:1 split. Once lower item tiers are exhausted, the chance of nothing is reduced instead.
- Improved odds persist after a successful purchase.

# Shrine of the Woods

- The shrine still creates a healing zone. Each purchase cycles an eligible equipped skill for the current run. The name and colour show the affected slot.
- Captain's two beacons, MUL-T's second primary and Acolyte's special or Litany have separate options.

| Variant | Colour | Skill slot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shrine of Instinct | Purple | Passive |
| Shrine of the First Strike | Blue | Primary |
| Shrine of the Echo | Orange | Secondary |
| Shrine of Passage | Green | Utility |
| Shrine of Fate | Pink | Special |

# Shrine Of Combat

- Defeating the shrine encounter awards item rewards.
- Summoned enemies drop **50%** of their normal gold.
- Reward rarity weights are 65% Common, 34% Uncommon and 1% Legendary.

# Void Seed Limit

- Adds 1 to the limit of void seeds on a stage per completed loop and per 5 Void item stacks held by the team, up to a maximum of 10.

# Wandering Chef

- Multiple players can use the cooking menu at the same time.
- Cooked meals go directly into the cooking player's inventory. Equipment still drops normally.

# Aurelionite Fragments

- Removes guaranteed Seekers of the Storm item choices. Eligible Storm items remain in the normal reward pool.

# Green Portals

- Completing a Halcyon Shrine encounter always opens a green portal, including on stages 4 and 5.
- Green portals used after stage 3 lead to Prime Meridian.
- Optional: require the full route through Reformed Altar and Treeborn Colony (or its loop variant) before Prime Meridian, regardless of the stage where the route starts. Off by default.
