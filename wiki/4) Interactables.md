# Teleporter

- Increases the holdout radius to 90 metres.
- After the boss is defeated, the radius expands to cover effectively the whole map and charging becomes twice as fast.

# Shrine Of Blood

- Awards gold equal to the health lost, plus the current cost of one Small Chest.

# Shrine Of Chance

- Starting rewards are 50% nothing, 40% Common, 9% Uncommon and 1% Legendary; equipment is removed from the pool.
- Failures improve reward quality. While Common items remain above 1%, 10 percentage points move from Common into Uncommon and Legendary in a 9:1 split. Once lower item tiers are exhausted, the chance of nothing is reduced instead.
- Improved odds persist after a successful purchase.

# Shrine Of Combat

- Defeating the shrine encounter awards item rewards.
- Reward rarity weights are 65% Common, 34% Uncommon and 1% Legendary.

# Void Seed Limit

- Raises the Void Seed limit to 3.
- Adds 1 to the limit per completed loop and per 5 Void item stacks held by the team, up to a maximum of 10.

# Wandering Chef

- Multiple players can use the cooking menu at the same time.
- Cooked meals go directly into the cooking player's inventory. Equipment still drops normally.

# Aurelionite Fragments

- Removes guaranteed Seekers of the Storm item choices. Eligible Storm items remain in the normal reward pool.

# Green Portals

- Completing a Halcyon Shrine encounter always opens a green portal, including on stages 4 and 5.
- Green portals used after stage 3 lead to Prime Meridian.
- Optional: require the full route through Reformed Altar and Treeborn Colony (or its loop variant) before Prime Meridian, regardless of the stage where the route starts. Off by default.
