# Regenerating Scrap

- Can also be used in Common 3D Printers.

# AtG Missile Mk. 1

- Missiles have a 0 proc coefficient (down from 1).

# Berzerker's Pauldron

- Triggers after 2 consecutive kills within 1 second (down from 4).
- Frenzy lasts 6 seconds without extra duration from stacks.
- Each frenzy stack grants **15%** movement speed and **30%** attack speed (down from **50%** and **100%**).
- Repeated activations stack up to 3 (+2 per additional item) times and refresh existing stacks.

# Box of Dynamite

- Gains **120%** damage per additional stack (up from **85%**).

# Chance Doll

- Successful Chance Shrines have a **20% (+20% per stack)** chance to duplicate their reward instead of upgrading it. Each full **100%** chance guarantees another copy, with the remainder rolled normally.

# Collector's Compulsion

- Pickups grant **0.5%** (+**0.25%** per additional item) to all stats, including acceleration, for the rest of the stage.
- Buff stacks are unlimited.
- Barrels and temporary items grant half the bonus.
- Lunar Coins count; equipment, printer items, scrap and item/skill collectibles do not count by default.

# Death Mark

- Requires 1 distinct debuff (down from 4), increasing damage taken by **10%** for each debuff instead of a flat **50%**. Death Mark itself does not count.

# Chronobauble

- Also reduces attack speed by **20%**.

# Harvester's Scythe

- Critical hits heal for 4 (+2 per stack), down from 8 (+4), plus **5% (+2% per stack)** of damage dealt.

# Hunter's Harpoon

- Grants **5%** critical chance. Critical hits reduce skill cooldowns by 0.5 (+0.25 per stack) seconds, multiplied by the triggering attack's proc coefficient, instead of granting speed on kill.

# Ignition Tank

- Hits have a **10%** chance to ignite for **50%** total damage. Grants **100% (+100% per stack)** extra fire damage instead of **300%** extra burn damage per stack. Stronger-burn upgrades remain.

# Infusion

- Has no health cap (formerly 100 per stack). Every 100 gained health adds one kill to the requirement for the next health award. Allies that inherit Infusion share health and kill progress with their owner.

# Kjaro's Band / Runald's Band

- Deal **200%** total damage per stack (down from **300%** and **250%**).

# Leeching Seed

- Heals when dealing damage over time instead of ordinary hits. Each damage tick heals for 1 health and **1%** of damage dealt per item stack, without proc-coefficient scaling.

# Lepton Daisy

- Pulses every 20 (-1 per additional stack) seconds during charging holdout zones instead of at charge milestones. Each pulse heals **50%** (+**10%** per additional stack) maximum health, with excess healing converted to barrier.

# Luminous Shot

- Each explosion deals **200% (+100% per stack)** total damage, up from **175%** (+**50%**).

# Old Guillotine

- Deals **50% (+25% per stack)** bonus damage to targets below **50%** combined health instead of executing low-health elites.

# Old War Stealthkit

- Damage has a **5%** chance to trigger invisibility, rising with hit size and reaching **100%** at **70%** of maximum combined health. Recharges in 10 seconds (down from 30), reduced by **50%** per additional stack.

# Prayer Beads

- Stored stats gain 10 percentage points per additional stack (up from 5).
- Previously consumed bead levels no longer reduce experience gain.
- Additional items grant the intended **25%** extra bead XP per stack without rounding the multiplier down to whole numbers.

# Razorwire

- Gains **40%** damage per additional stack. Its proc coefficient is 0 (down from 0.5).

# Red Whip

- Also grants **10%** movement speed during combat.

# Sale Star

- Gains 1 additional use per stage for each extra stack. Each chest consumes one star. Extra random item chances from stacking are removed.

# Squid Polyp

- Lasts 30 (+5 per additional stack) seconds, using a fixed lifetime instead of health drain. Applies tar and gains **20%** health per additional stack.
- During an actively charging holdout, summons a Squid Turret near the centre every **15 seconds** per living holder by default. The interval and its scaling are configurable.

# War Horn

- Grants **60% (+15% per stack)** attack speed for 6 (+3 per stack) seconds, formerly **70%** for 8 (+4) seconds.

# Will-o'-the-wisp

- Its on-kill explosion has a 0 proc coefficient (down from 1).
