# Radar Scanner

- Cooldown is **20** seconds (down from 45 seconds).

# Molotov (6-Pack)

- Deals **500%** damage per explosion (up from **250%**), matching its description. Burning areas deal **200%** damage per second.

# Jade Elephant

- Cooldown is **30** seconds.

# Goobo Jr.

- Inherits your items, with **100%** health and damage. Equipment is not inherited.

# Gorag’s Opus

- Frenzy lasts **10** seconds.

# Milky Chrysalis

- Cooldown is **45** seconds.

# Ocular HUD

- Critical chance granted above **100%** converts into critical strike damage at a 1:1 ratio.

# Preon Accumulator

- Cooldown is **120** seconds.
- Lightning deals **300%** damage every **0.25** seconds, with a **0.5** proc coefficient.
- The description correctly shows **8000%** explosion damage.
- Explosion proc coefficient is **2**. *The lower lightning damage prevents it from triggering the bands.*

# The Back-up

- Ignores the normal ally cap, with a separate limit of 4 active Strike Drones per player. Enfilade (the drones' primary) fires 4 shots for **75%** damage each (up from **50%**).

# The Crowdfunder

- Bullets deal **200%** damage. (Up from **100%**)

# Executive Card

- Keeps unbought multishop terminals open for everyone while a player holds the card.
- Gaining a card can reopen previously closed multishops and make their unbought terminals available. This mod hands retroactive reopening to RetroactiveMacro when installed.
- Gold purchases still grant **10% cashback** by default. Cashback and the two multishop changes are configurable.

# Recycler

- Cooldown is capped at 1 second while the run timer is paused. Uses in this mode do not trigger equipment-use effects.

# Elite Aspects

- Just a note, equipment settings for these are unchanged by default but if you do change them, they yield to ZetAspects when installed.
