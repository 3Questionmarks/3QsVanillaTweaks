# Defense Nucleus

- Extra stacks grant constructs **+150%** damage and health (up from +**100%**) instead of increasing the four-construct limit. Constructs count as mechanical allies. Summoning at the limit replaces the oldest construct.

# Halcyon Seed

- Each extra seed grants Aurelionite **+100%** damage, replacing square-root damage scaling.

# Planula

- Heals **10 (+10 per stack)** health plus **3% (+3% per stack)** maximum health when damaged, replacing **15 (+15 per stack)** flat healing.

# Queen’s Gland

- Extra stacks grant the guard **+300%** damage and **+100%** health instead of summoning additional guards.

# Titanic Knurl

- Additionally grants **10% (+10% per stack)** maximum health. Keeps its flat health and regeneration.

# Mired Urn

- Siphons **3 (+1 per stack)** targets, up from **1 (+1 per stack)**. Deals **25% (+10% per stack)** damage every **0.25** seconds, replacing **100%** damage per second without damage stacking.

# Functional Coupler

- Supported equipment passives remain active in extra slots, including elite aspects, Seed of Life, Gnarled Woodsprite and Executive Card.

# Prison Matrix

- Its armour bonus applies to total armour instead of only base armour.

# Irradiant Pearl

- Multiplies total armour and grants an actual **10%** regeneration increase per stack. Does not multiply negative regeneration by default.

# Charged Perforator, Little Disciple, Molten Perforator, Mycelium Cap, and Shatterspleen

- Their proc coefficients have been reduced to **0**.
