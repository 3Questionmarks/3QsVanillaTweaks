# Chronic Expansion

- Each kill grants **4% (+2% per stack)** bonus damage, up from **3.5%** (+**1%**).

# Crowbar

- Deals **50% (+50% per stack)** bonus damage, down from **75%** (+**75%**).
- Applies to the holder's **first hit against each target**, requiring a non-zero proc coefficient, instead of targets above **90%** health.
- Bonus damage also strengthens on-hit procs. Vanilla health-threshold behaviour remains configurable.

# Bundle of Fireworks

- Launches **8 fireworks**, with no additional fireworks per stack (vanilla: +4).
- Each deals **400% (+200% per stack)** damage, up from **300%**.
- Proc coefficient is **0**.

# Bolstering Lantern

- Grants **10% attack speed per nearby body**.
- Additional item stacks add **5% attack speed per nearby body**, up from **3.5%**.
- Removes the duplicate attack-speed contribution from nearby bodies.

# Bustling Fungus

- Heals **5% (+2.5% per stack)** maximum health per second, up from **4.5%** (+**2.25%**).
- Radius is **5m (+2.5m per stack)**.

# Delicate Watch

- Defaults to the **wear-down rework**: grants **15% (+15% per stack)** damage instead of vanilla's **20%** (+**20%**).
- Each damaging hit removes **one percentage point** of the damage bonus.
- Bleed, burn, other damage-over-time ticks and void fog do not wear or break watches by default. Fall damage is excluded by a separate setting. Both exclusions apply to the wear-down rework.
- Being hit while the remaining bonus is at or below **5% (+5% per stack)** breaks the watches.
- Fully repairs at the start of the next stage, resetting wear and restoring broken copies.
- Disabling the rework uses **15% (+15% per stack)** damage and the vanilla **25%** health break threshold.

# Delicate Watch (Broken)

- Grants **2.5% (+2.5% per stack)** damage.
- Can be scrapped and regenerates at the start of the next stage.

# Eclipse Lite

- Grants **0.5% (+0.5% per stack)** barrier per second of the recharged skill's base cooldown, instead of **1%** (+**0.25%**).

# Oddly-shaped Opal

- Description clarifies that **7 seconds without taking damage** grants **100 (+100 per stack) armour** until the next hit. Mechanics are unchanged by default.

# Sticky Bomb

- Deals **240%** total damage, up from **180%**.

# Stun Grenade

- Uses **linear chance scaling**: **5% (+5% per stack)**, scaled by the triggering hit's proc coefficient. Vanilla uses hyperbolic scaling.
- Successful procs also deal **100% base damage**, with a **0 proc coefficient**.

# Topaz Brooch

- Grants **10 flat barrier**, with no flat increase per additional stack, instead of 15 (+15).
- Also grants **1% (+1% per stack)** maximum combined health as barrier on kill.

# Warbanner

- Also grants **10% damage, 3 health regeneration, 10 armour and 10% critical chance**. Vanilla's **30%** movement and attack speed bonuses remain.
- Buffs linger for **2 seconds** after leaving the radius.
- Spawns banners for supported holdout zones and when a final boss spawns.
- Engineer turrets and inheriting proliferation offshoots (as well as other abilities that inherit) can inherit it.
- Limited to **3 active banners per player**, shared with inheriting minions. New banners replace the oldest.

# Power Elixir

- Restores **25% health** and grants **50% maximum combined health as barrier**, instead of healing **75%**.
- Still activates at **25% combined health** after taking damage.

# Power Elixir (Consumed)

- Taking damage restores **0.5% (+0.5% per stack)** health and grants **1% (+1% per stack)** maximum combined health as barrier.
- Can be scrapped and regenerates at the start of the next stage.
