# Lost Seer's Lenses

- Replaces instant kills with Void Crits: a **1%** chance per stack to deal **1000%** of the triggering hit's damage.
- Void Crits can affect bosses, benefit from luck and critical-damage multipliers, and trigger on-crit effects.

# Pluripotent Larva

- Consumed copies regenerate at the start of the next stage.

# Benthic Bloom

- Swaps its positive effect with Purity by default. It reduces skill cooldowns by **2 seconds (+1 second per extra stack)** and still corrupts 57 Leaf Clover.
- Turning off **Swap Benthic Bloom And Purity Effects** restores Bloom's configurable item upgrades. See **Purity** on the Lunar items page for the upgrade rules.

# Newly Hatched Zoea

- Additional stacks give allies **100%** bonus damage instead of increasing the ally limit or reducing the summon cooldown. Defaults to one ally and a 60-second cooldown at every stack count.

# Plasma Shrimp

- Missiles deal **50% (+25% per stack)** total damage, from **40%** (+**40%**). Missile proc coefficient is 0.

# Polylute

- Adds configurable values, including a proc coefficient of 0.2 by default.

# Singularity Band

- Deals **300% (+200% per stack)** total damage, from **100%** (+**100%**). Cooldown is 15 seconds, down from 20.

# Voidsent Flame

- Activates on your first hit against each enemy instead of requiring full health.
- Deals **320% (+160% per stack)** base damage, from **260%** (+**156%**).
- Explosion proc coefficient is 0.
- With first-hit mode disabled, activates above **90%** combined health by default; setting this to **100%** requires full combined health.
