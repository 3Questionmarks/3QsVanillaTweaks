# Egocentrism

- Consumes an item every 120 seconds instead of 60, with 2 seconds removed per additional stack, down to a minimum of 10 seconds.
- Prioritises White, Green, Yellow and Red Scrap, then Lunar items, before other eligible items. Void items are excluded.
- Consumption pauses whenever the run timer is paused.

# Gesture of the Drowned

- Reduces equipment cooldowns by **25%** per stack (from **50%**, then **15%** per additional stack).
- Each additional stack adds a **10%** chance for equipment to fail, spending a charge without activating equipment or equipment-use effects.
- Automatic use remains disabled in the Bazaar and Computational Exchange.

# Visions of Heresy - Hungering Gaze

- Hungering Gaze's initial hit deals **5%** damage with a 0.1 proc coefficient.
- Projectile tracking is twice as aggressive.
- These changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Hooks of Heresy - Slicing Maelstrom

- With detailed skill descriptions enabled, Slicing Maelstrom shows **175% damage every 0.2 seconds** instead of damage per second.
- The description changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Strides of Heresy - Shadowfade

- Shadowfade heals **1.5%** of maximum health every 0.2 seconds.
- Activating Shadowfade cleanses debuffs, damage over time and stun, while preserving cooldown effects such as bands.
- The description shows the healing ticks rather than vanilla's misleading total.
- These changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Essence of Heresy - Ruin

- Reapplying Ruin refreshes the duration of existing stacks.
- The description explicitly includes Agile and corrects Ruin's formatting for Moffein's Heretic.
- These changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Stone Flux Pauldron

- Replaces the movement-speed penalty with 50 armour lost per stack.

# Brittle Crown

- Gold chance gains 1 percentage point for every **10%** attack damage above **100%**.

# Defiant Gouge

- Defeating all summoned enemies drops an item.
- Rewards have **55%** common, **35%** uncommon, **1%** legendary and **9%** lunar odds.
- Each additional stack gives a **10%** chance for another reward; chances above **100%** roll for even more items. For example 150% would always drop 2 items, and drop 3 50% of the time.
- Also works alongside Shrine of Combat rewards.
- Failed Chance Shrine purchases still summon enemies but give no Gouge reward.
- Summoned enemies drop no gold by default.

# Longstanding Solitude

- Gaining a level grants 1 free purchase per item stack, without the vanilla cap of 3 purchases per level.
- Gold still becomes experience: one difficulty-scaled small chest's gold cost grants **12%** of the experience needed for the next level.
- Removes the stacking experience bonus; each additional stack instead increases the gold needed for the same converted experience by **25%**.
- **Note:** This now actually has a downside to stacking and doesn't just fuck over your friends by doubling thier purchacing costs.

# Neutronium Weight

- Reworked completly
- Reduces damage, attack speed and movement speed by **20% (+10% per stack)**.
- No longer transfers on hit or death.
- Removing it grants **Neutronium Weight (Released)**, permanently increasing these stats by **10% (+5% per stack)**.

# Transcendence

- Increases maximum health by **50% (+50% per stack)**, up from **50%** (+**25%**).
- Normal one-shot protection is disabled while shield gating is enabled.
- Each additional stack increases shield replenishment time by 1 second.
- **Note:** This now actually has a downside, and stacking it isn't free.
