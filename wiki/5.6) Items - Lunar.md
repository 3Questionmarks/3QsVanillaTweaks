# Egocentrism

- Consumes an item every 120 seconds instead of 60, with 2 seconds removed per additional stack, down to a minimum of 10 seconds.
- Prioritises White, Green, Yellow and Red Scrap, then Lunar items, before other eligible items. Void items are excluded.
- Consumption pauses whenever the run timer is paused.

# Gesture Of The Drowned

- Stops automatically activating equipment in the Bazaar Between Time and Computational Exchange. Manual activation and equipment recharging still work.

# Visions of Heresy - Hungering Gaze

- Hungering Gaze's initial hit deals 5% damage with a 0.1 proc coefficient.
- Projectile tracking is twice as aggressive.
- These changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Hooks of Heresy - Slicing Maelstrom

- Corrects Slicing Maelstrom's description to show 175% damage every 0.2 seconds: 875% damage per second.
- The description changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Strides of Heresy - Shadowfade

- Shadowfade heals 1.5% of maximum health every 0.2 seconds.
- Activating Shadowfade cleanses debuffs, damage over time and stun, while preserving cooldown effects such as bands.
- The description shows the healing ticks rather than vanilla's misleading total.
- These changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Essence of Heresy - Ruin

- Reapplying Ruin refreshes the duration of existing stacks.
- The description explicitly includes Agile and corrects Ruin's formatting for Moffein's Heretic.
- These changes also apply to Moffein's Heretic.

# Stone Flux Pauldron

- Replace the movement-speed penalty with 50 armour lost per stack.
