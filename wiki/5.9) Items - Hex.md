These changes are primarally focused on making the hexes not effect other players. They should only now effect the holder.

# Hex of Rain

- The holder takes **20%** more damage and deals **20%** less damage per stack instead of increasing run difficulty. Extra stacks add diminishing penalties.

# Hex of Patience

- Only hurts the holder outside the charging teleporter zone, using their own stacks rather than the team's total.

# Hex of Strife

- The holder takes **30%** more damage from bosses and deals **30%** less damage to bosses per stack instead of increasing teleporter boss difficulty. Extra stacks add diminishing penalties.

# Hex of Greed

- Reduces the holder's gold income by **25%** per stack instead of sharing items with enemies. The reduction also applies before Longstanding Solitude converts gold into experience. Extra stacks add diminishing penalties.

# Hex of Haunting / Hex of Mimicry

- Spawned Poltergeists and Mimics can only target their holder.

# Hex of Loss

- Permanently removes **1 item copy per Hex stack** when the Teleporter begins charging. 
- Prioritises lower-tier scrap, consuming ordinary scrap before Regenerating Scrap of the same tier.
- Lunar items and important items such as Prison Matrix and the Cerebellums are protected by default. Temporary items are excluded.
