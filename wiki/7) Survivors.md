# Commando

- **Phase Round:** adds 50% damage per enemy pierced (up from 40%) and travels 50% faster.
- **Tactical Dive:** fully reloads the primary, mainly useful with Visions of Heresy.
- **Tactical Slide:** cooldown is 3 seconds (down from 4).
- **Suppressive Fire:** fires 8 base shots (up from 6).
- **Frag Grenade:** can be cooked and has a 2-second fuse.
- **Sabotage:** has 3 stocks, a 3-second cooldown and throws one explosive per use (instead of 1 stock throwing 3 explosives with a 7-second cooldown).

# Huntress

- Adds **Momentum**, a passive granting up to 20% critical strike chance through sustained sprinting. Taking health damage resets it; barrier can protect the stacks.
- **Strafe:** deals 170% damage (up from 150%).
- **Laser Glaive:** gains 20% damage per bounce (up from 10%).
- **Laser Cyclone:** reworked to have 1 stock and a 7-second cooldown, dealing 200% damage every 0.2 seconds. It can no longer be juggled.
- **Blink:** has a 6-second cooldown and reduced movement-speed scaling.
- **Phase Blink:** has reduced movement-speed scaling.
- **Arrow Rain:** deals 100% damage every 0.25 seconds for 8 seconds (400% per second, up from 330%). Its radius is greatly increased.
- Targeting prioritises the enemy nearest the crosshair. Strafe gains 20 metres of lock-on range, and Laser Glaive uses the primary's targeting range.

# Bandit

- Hemorrhage ignores armour. Critical primary hits can now inflict it: 5% per Burst pellet or 25% for Blast.
- **Burst:** fires 5 pellets. Supports hold-to-fire.
- **Blast:** deals 400% (up from 340%) damage and pierces 2 enemies (vanilla does not pierce). Supports hold-to-fire.
- **Serrated Dagger:** gains a short lunge and a 50% larger hitbox. No longer counts as in-combat when using.
- **Serrated Shiv:** Hitbox is doubled. No longer counts as in-combat when using.
- **Lights Out / Desperado:** use a 6-second cooldown (up from 4) and grant kill rewards if the target dies within 0.5 seconds of the hit (vanilla requires the shot itself to kill).
- **Desperado:** gains 1% extra damage per Bandit Skull (down from 10%); stacks persist through death and stage transitions instead of resetting.

# MUL-T

- **Rebar Puncher:** deals 700% damage. (Up from 600%)
- **Scrap Launcher:** deals 400% damage (Up from 360%), and faster projectiles. Gains Pocket I.C.B.M. support.
- **Power-Saw:** now converts 20% of damage dealt into barrier.
- **Blast Canister:** releases 6 bomblets dealing 44% damage each. (Up from 4)
- **Retool:** also reloads primaries.
- **Power Mode:** also grants 50% bonus damage.

# Engineer

- **Bouncing Grenades:** charges up to 15 grenades. (Up from 8)
- **Pressure Mines:** arms in 3 seconds and deals 1500% armed damage (Up from 900%).
- **Bubble Shield:** has a 30-second cooldown (Up from 25s), lasts 25 seconds (up from 15s) and allows up to 3 active shields (up from 1). Enemies are pushed out of the shield's area when it deploys. Allies inside, including turrets and drones, gain 100 armour, 10 health regeneration per second and 15% attack speed. Overlapping shields do not stack.
- **Thermal Harpoons:** has 4 stocks, deals 700% damage and distributes targets evenly instead of concentrating them on one target. Missiles take 0.2 seconds to lock onto a target rather than locking immediately when it enters the sight.
- **TR12 Gauss Auto-Turret:** gains an 80-metre targeting range and 200-metre bullet range.
- **TR58 Carbonizer Turret:** always sprints, locks its aim to targets more reliably and gains increased targeting range.

# Artificer

- **Flame Bolt / Plasma Bolt:** deal 300% damage. (Up from 280%)
- **Charged Nano-Bomb / Cast Nano-Spear:** While charging, artificer doesn't fall and hovers.
- **Flamethrower:** can be used while sprinting, reaches 30 metres, always ignites and can be cancelled early by pressing the skill again.
- **Ion Surge:** Removes movement-speed scaling from its launch, and protects the first landing from fall damage for 5 seconds.
- With Artificer Extended installed, adds **Voltaic Surge** using the Ion Surge settings, leaving Ion Surge unchanged.

# Mercenary

- Kills now restore the extra jump.
- Reduces movement-speed scaling on movement skills.
- **Blinding Assault:** removes the limit on repeat dashes.
- **Focused Assault:** gains Slayer damage and unlimited resets on kills.
- **Eviscerate:** lasts 2 seconds. (up from 1.5)

# REX

- Adds **Adaptive Plating** as an alternative passive: each 1% missing health grants 1 armour and 1% damage.
- **Natural Toxins:** now directly causes all damage done to inflict weaken 50% of the time. Skills that used to weaken, no longer do.
- Health costs no longer cause permanent damage in Eclipse 8.
- **Directive Inject:** all 3 shots heal; each deals 100% damage and heals for 40% of damage dealt. (Up from 80% damage and a 60% heal on the final shot only.)
- **Directive Disperse:** cooldown is 4.5 seconds. (Down from 5.5s) (Still weakens enemies directly since it doesn't do damage)
- **Bramble Volley:** heals based on enemies' maximum health rather than REX's.
- **Proliferation:** flowers now inherit items. Flower teleportation has a 5.5-second cooldown, costs the flower 30% maximum health and heals REX for 10% maximum health. (instead of killing the flower and healing 20%)
- **DIRECTIVE: Harvest:** deals 600% damage (up from 330%).
- **Tangling Growth:** heals for 2% of each rooted target's maximum health per second instead of scaling healing from REX's health. Healing stops when the target dies, awarding 20% of its maximum health immediately instead. 

# Loader

- **Grapple Fist:** has 2 stocks.
- **M551 Pylon:** lasts 16.65 seconds (up from 14s), zaps every 0.7 seconds. (Up from every 1 second)

# Acrid

- **Regenerative:** counts as healing, instead of regeneration, allowing healing-related effects to apply.
- **Vicious Wounds:** has a 50% larger hitbox.
- **Ravenous Bite:** deals 400% damage. (up from 320%)
- Both leaps use reduced movement-speed scaling and remove landing damage falloff.
- **Frenzied Leap:** has an 8-second cooldown and refunds 2 seconds per enemy hit. (Down from 10 second cooldown)
- **Cloying Breath:** has a 10-second cooldown (down from 12s) and deals 80% damage every 0.25 seconds for 6.5 seconds. (Up from 60% damage)
- Poison and Blight ignore armour, and refresh their duration when reapplied. Remove the damage cap from both as well.

# Captain

- **Vulcan Shotgun:** fires uncharged shots while held. Adds **Charged Shotgun** as a secondary, reaching 200% damage per pellet at full charge with no damage falloff.
- **Power Tazer:** has a 4-second cooldown (down from 5s), bounces up to 3 times (up from once) and slows targets.
- **Orbital Probe:** cooldown is 9 seconds (down from 11s). Orbital skills work in Hidden Realms.
- **Supply Beacons:** recharge every 60 seconds, allow up to 3 active beacons and count as holdout zones.
- **Healing Beacon:** now also grants 30% attack speed and 25 armour.
- **Shocking Beacon:** now deals 350% damage (up from 0%), can critically strike and slows enemies.
- **Resupply Beacon:** regenerates uses instead of permanently consuming them; a use recharges every 10 seconds, restores all skill slots and grants 50% maximum health as barrier.
- **Hacking Beacon:** reduces costs by 50%, can hack printers and all multishop segments, and has a 15% chance to replace a hacked reward with a Command choice.

# Railgunner

- **Magnetic Accelerator:** grants 2% extra critical damage per 1% critical chance (up from 1%).
- **XQR Smart Round System:** projectiles travel 50% faster and no longer push Railgunner backwards while grounded.
- **HH44 Marksman:** each Backup Magazine grants 25% extra shot damage. Vanilla gives Marksman no benefit from Backup Magazines.
- **M99 Sniper:** the perfect-reload bonus applies to every shot before the next reload instead of only the first.
- **Concussion Device:** deals 50% damage to enemies, uses Sweet Spot falloff and has a 0.1 proc coefficient. It cannot damage Railgunner by default; its launch force is preserved.
- **Polar Field Device:** now also weakens enemies, and applies Frost every second.
- **Cryocharge:** can execute enemies brought below the frozen execution threshold immediately, rather than requiring a follow-up shot to do so.

# Void Fiend

- Corruption no longer changes from taking damage, critical hits or healing. It builds at 1.5% per second and 5% per kill.
- **Flood:** now allows other abilities while charging; cooldown starts after firing.
- Both Trespass forms use reduced movement-speed scaling and protect the first landing from fall damage for 5 seconds.
- **Corrupted Suppress:** has unlimited uses, allowing repeated exchanges of health for corruption.

# Seeker

- **Soul Spiral:** grants barrier based on current maximum health instead of level-one health.
- **Meditate:** Heals allies for 10% (up from 4%), plus 5% per Tranquility stack (up from 2%).

# False Son

- Growth now also counts shield at 50% effectiveness.
- **Club of the Forsaken:** fixes the falling-slam proc coefficient setting the charged club attack proc coefficent to 1.5.
- Both utility skills can be used while sprinting and alongside Club or Laser attacks. Movement-speed range scaling is reduced to 50%.
- **Laser of the Father:** base damage increased and proc coeficent increased to 0.7. Can be hled for 2 seconds longer at base.

# CHEF

- Fixes suspended Dice damage and proc behaviour.
- **Ice Box:** the boosted version deals 2000% damage. (Up from 1500%)
- **Roll:** Movement-speed distance scaling is reduced to 50% (from 100%).
- **Yes CHEF:** deals 600% damage. (Up from 400%)

# Operator

- **Admin-Override, Ascent Protocol, Ejection Core and Amp Core:** can be used while sprinting.
- **Firewall:** blocks sprinting while held, avoiding unwanted releases from auto-sprint mods.
- **Ejection Core:** Nano-Bugs gain 100% extra skill damage; split hits deal up to 120% base damage.
- **Amp Core:** stores up to 2000% damage and reaches a 20-metre blast radius.

# Drifter

- **Repossess:** keeps bagged objects between stages; allied players can escape after 12 seconds (time to throw your friends off the map).
- **Bludgeon:** deals 350% damage. **Discard:** deals 300% damage.
- **Tornado Slam:** has a 7-second cooldown (down from 8), deals 300% per hit and 900% on the final slam, and fixes attack-speed scaling.
- **Salvage:** consumes all junk and creates one item per 2 junk consumed instead of a fixed 4 items. It cannot be used in the Bazaar or Computational Exchange.
- **Tinker:** has a 20-second cooldown and can now downgrade scrap into 2 copies of the lower tier.

# Enforcer

- **Shrapnel Grenade:** gains Tracer support.
- **Surge Protection:** deals 250% base damage, gains damage from barrier and shield, and has a minimum 20-metre blast radius.
- **Ripcord:** fixes taunt targeting and increases taunt duration to 4 seconds (from 1).
- **Rally Point:** has a 45-second cooldown (up from 24s), lasts 30 seconds (up from 16s) and reaches 40 metres.
- **Crash Point:** Fixes its landing damage category.

# Acolyte

- **N'kuhana's Grace:** grants 2% attack speed per 1% maximum health held as recoverable health (Up from 1%).
- **Soul Jar:** has a 5-second cooldown. Haunt deals 25% damage every 0.5 seconds (instead of 16.66% every 0.333 seconds). Wights seek targets within 128 metres (up from 40) and deal 50% damage (down from 100%).
- **Bola Throw:** returns 200% of damage dealt to extracted souls. (up from 100%)
- **Blade Sling:** now stuns on hit as the description implied.
- **Blade Dash:** deals 400% damage, as the description stated. (up from 300%)
- **Anima Echo:** now refreshes cooldowns and grants 20 armour and 50% personal damage (both bonuses were 0); echo damage now matches the base damage of the attacks, instead of being reduced by roughly 50%.

# Heretic

- New Passive 'Nevermore'
- Prevents Heresy items from being removed and removes their negative stacking penalties.
- Allows Shadowfade to be cancelled early and fixes regeneration multipliers.
- With Moffein's Heretic installed - Compatibility base stats default to 110 HP, 12 damage and 1 HP regeneration.
- Heresy skill changes are listed on the four Heresy item pages.
