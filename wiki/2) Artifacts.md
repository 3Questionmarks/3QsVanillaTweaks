# Artifact of Command

- Drops the item directly when a Command cube would contain only one choice.

# Artifact of the Fleeting Moon

- Adds an optional artifact that replaces spendable Lunar Coins with a temporary per-run balance, starting at 5 coins player.
- Multiplies Lunar Coin drops by 5.
- Every 5 collected coins also earns one permanent Lunar Coin. Existing saved coins remain separate.
- By default, in multiplayer, players share these coins.
- Bazaar unlock mode activates during Fleeting Moon by default.
- Additional Objectives also award lunar coins. These do not count toward the permanent pickup reward.

| Objective | Temporary Lunar Coins |
| --- | ---: |
| Defeat a boss | 3 |
| Complete a stage after collecting all its items | 2 |
| Gain 1 Karma | 5 |
